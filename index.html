Architect Radio Publisher

Architect Radio Publisher is the internal publishing tool of Architect Radio, a one-person technology-education brand. It runs as a command-line program on the owner's own computer and uploads the owner's finished videos to the owner's own YouTube channel, AI for Builders, as private or scheduled videos with their full metadata: title, description, tags, playlist, captions, custom thumbnail, AI-content disclosure and Hindi localizations. It then checks each upload field by field and collects the channel's own reach and analytics reports. It is not a public service, has no other users and never accesses anyone else's channel.
